# Code of conduct

We want this project to be a welcoming place to learn, build, and collaborate.

Be respectful, assume good intent, give actionable feedback, and make room for people with different
backgrounds and experience levels. Harassment, personal attacks, discrimination, sexualized
content, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project spaces.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, commits, issues, and other contributions that violate
these expectations, and may temporarily or permanently restrict participation when necessary.

Report conduct concerns privately to `jadhavparth99@gmail.com`. Reports will be reviewed promptly
and handled as confidentially as practical. This policy applies in the repository and in public
spaces where someone is representing the project.
